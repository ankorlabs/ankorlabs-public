Oatmeal Notes.

Disposable notes for your desktop. Open it and you get a fresh note. It stays wherever you leave it until you close it, and closing it deletes it for good.

macOS 12 or newer

Mac

oatmeal-notes-mac.zip · 87 KB · Apple silicon & Intel

  1. Unzip and drag Oatmeal Notes into Applications.
  2. Open it. The first time, macOS blocks it because it isn’t from the App Store.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway. Only needed once.
Download for Mac

Windows 10 or 11

Windows

oatmeal-notes-windows.zip · 5 KB · no admin needed

  1. Right-click the zip and choose Extract All.
  2. Double-click Install.cmd. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info → Run anyway.
  3. It builds the app and adds Oatmeal Notes to the Start menu.
Download for Windows

How it works

  • Every open makes a new note. ⌘N / Ctrl+N makes another.
  • Move and resize notes however you like. They’re normal windows, so other windows can cover them.
  • Every 5 minutes your notes fade back in on top. The pause button in a note’s corner turns that off.
  • Notes survive a restart or crash. Open the app and they come back where you left them.
  • Close a note and it’s gone. Nothing is uploaded anywhere; notes live only on your computer.
